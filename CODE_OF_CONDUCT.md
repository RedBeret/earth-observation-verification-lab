# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome constructive review,
focus criticism on the work, and protect personal and sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or deliberate disclosure of credentials
or private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates this standard.
Security concerns should follow the private reporting guidance in `SECURITY.md`.
